# 04 Demo

The submission needs a 3-minute video showing only what was built during the hackathon. It is also what the judges watch first, so it is planned before the code.

## The 3 minutes

| Time | Shot | Point it makes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:10 | A phone photographs an old windmill | The input is just one photo |
| 0:10 to 0:55 | The finished film plays, full screen, vertical | The wow first (Demo 25%) |
| 0:55 to 1:25 | The pipeline, step by step on screen: SAM cut-out, research with live citations, verification dropping a claim | Use of Meta tooling (25%), technical depth (20%) |
| 1:25 to 1:50 | The same object in every era, pixel-identical, side by side | The object is real, not generated |
| 1:50 to 2:20 | Two more films, short cuts: an old tree, a lighthouse | It generalises |
| 2:20 to 2:45 | Who it is for: a heritage plaque with a QR code, a visitor watching | Impact (30%) |
| 2:45 to 3:00 | End card: sources, AI label, repo link | Honesty, and what was built |

## Rules the video must follow

- Show only work from 27 October to 4 November 2026.
- Name the Meta models on screen where they act.
- Every film shown carries its sources and the AI label.

## Also needed for submission

- Public repository, accessible.
- Video link, accessible.
- Optional: a post on X with the demo (Most Viral prize, engagement counted until 8 November).
