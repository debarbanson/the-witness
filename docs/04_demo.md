# 04 Demo

The submission needs a 3-minute video showing only what was built during the hackathon. It is also what the judges watch first, so it is planned before the code.

## The 3 minutes

| Time | Shot | Point it makes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:10 | A phone photographs an old windmill | The input is just one photo |
| 0:10 to 0:55 | The finished film plays, full screen, vertical | The wow first (Demo 25%) |
| 0:55 to 1:25 | The pipeline, step by step on screen: SAM cut-out, research with live citations, verification dropping a claim | Use of Meta tooling (25%), technical depth (20%) |
| 1:25 to 1:50 | The object's own life, side by side: De Adriaan built 1779, burnt 1932, gone for seventy years, rebuilt 2002, each with its source; then the check at work: the painter put tulips on the water tower 54 years early, the check caught it three times and the scene was dropped | It tells the object's true story, and checks what it shows |
| 1:50 to 2:10 | For children: the same object, adult and children's version side by side, then a child's hand playing spot the difference on a phone | One truth, two audiences; families and schools (Impact 30%, Creative Build) |
| 2:10 to 2:30 | Short cuts from other countries and languages (a lighthouse, a bridge in Japan, a tree) | It works anywhere |
| 2:30 to 2:45 | Who it is for, and what's next: a heritage plaque with a QR code; the witness hunt, a family walk collecting the films of nearby objects | Impact (30%) |
| 2:45 to 3:00 | End card: sources, AI label, repo link | Honesty, and what was built |

Delivery numbers, measured 28 Oct on De Adriaan: opening card 21.5 s after confirming, first painted scene 54.4 s, full film 172.9 s.

## Rules the video must follow

- Show only work from 27 October to 4 November 2026.
- Name the Meta models on screen where they act.
- Every film shown carries its sources and the AI label.

## Also needed for submission

- Public repository, accessible.
- Video link, accessible.
- Optional: a post on X with the demo (Most Viral prize, engagement counted until 8 November).
