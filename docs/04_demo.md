# 04 Demo script

English, at most 3:00 (judges stop at 3:00), no slides. The owner presents: a short on-camera opening by the owner, then the owner's voice-over over real screen recordings on a phone and a laptop, with English captions burned in. Everything shown is what the code does; the step list names the working Meta model live.

| Time | Picture | Voice-over |

| 0:00 to 0:15 | Owner on camera | "The Witness turns one photo of something old into a short film of the eras it lived through. Nothing existed before; it was built from scratch during the hackathon, from 26 Oct 2026." |

## Rules the video must follow

- Show only work from 26 October 2026 (kick-off 17:00 Dutch time) to 4 November 2026.
- Name the Meta models on screen where they act (the step list does this live).
- Every film shown carries its sources and the AI label.
- Mostly real screen footage; no slides; English captions burned in.
